### CWE-191: Integer Underflow or Wraparound

- Trace attainable values through subtraction, decrement, negation, or related arithmetic. Establish operand types, promotions, the value range crossing the representable minimum, and the visible consequence required by this rule.
- A visible operation whose operands are established in the supplied file to cross the minimum is sufficient, even without a later allocation or memory access. For unsigned subtraction, a known left operand smaller than the right establishes wraparound; for signed arithmetic, describe undefined behavior rather than assuming modulo arithmetic.
- Check lower-bound guards, widening or signedness conversions, checked arithmetic, constants, and compiler rules. Do not report a subtraction merely because one operand is variable.

#### Finding location

- The primary operation is the arithmetic expression whose mathematical result falls below the operation type's representable minimum. Explain the operand values, operation type, and resulting underflow or wraparound.
