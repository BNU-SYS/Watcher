# CWE-862: Missing Authorization

Report when a reachable restricted action omits a required permission, ownership or tenant decision.

1. Establish the acting principal, protected object/action and expected policy.
2. Trace identifiers to the operation and inspect applicable service checks, annotations, filters and route configuration.
3. Demonstrate a lower-privilege or cross-owner path that executes without the required decision. Check identity-scoped queries and ownership enforcement before access.

Locate the unguarded operation or exposing configuration. Effective service/middleware enforcement refutes the path. Missing local annotations or authentication alone supply insufficient evidence. Distinguish a missing decision from an incorrect implemented policy. Record essential unknown enforcement layers in `limitations`.
